What we heard

The brief

A mobile-first neighborhood reporting app. Keep the identity friendly and cartoonish without turning it into a mascot. The mark should read clearly as a report or message, and retain the selected three-dot speech-bubble idea.

friendlyclearplayfulapproachable

Keep the selected B speech bubble recognizable at small sizes, provide strong contrast, and preserve a real one-color cutout version.

Final design

Message Pop

A speech bubble frames three lively report dots, with a rounded Mona Sans Bold wordmark that keeps the brand playful without a character.

  • The bubble signals a report or message at a glance.
  • The orange dots add a pop of personality while the charcoal silhouette stays readable.
  • Mona Sans vector lettering keeps the full logo self-contained and font-free.
